What it does

Every card. Both sides. Findable.

Keep the inside, too

The message is the part that matters. Kept stores the front and the inside together, so the handwriting is never lost.

Scan in seconds

Photograph a card on the kitchen table and Kept finds the edges and crops it clean. No scanner, no fuss.

Find any card

Your whole collection in one place, sorted by date and by who sent it — and searchable by what’s written inside, because Kept reads the handwriting for you. Twenty years of birthdays, one scroll.
